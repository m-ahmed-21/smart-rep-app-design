# SmartRep: Your Smart Gym Companion

Your are a world-class professional front-end designer entrusted with designing a ground-breaking, modern, technological cross-platform mobile application: SmartRep, a Bluetooth-enabled real-time rep-tracking, range-of-motion visualization, and gym equipment/machine reservation system.

You have been provided with an MD file containing all important details of the application system, including hardware, connectivity, and the criteria for the front-end design (Proof of Concept). Create individual designs of each of the app pages/screens in accordance with what is defined in the MD file.

Additionally, you have been provided two SVG logos for the application. One is colorful, depicting the rating or feedback (visualization) feature of the software; whereas, the other one is monochrome, minimalistic, and more professional. When designing the PoC, that is the frontend of the application, you must carefully and thoughtfully select one from the two provided logos.

The overall app design should be similar or BETTER than the frontend designs of the following mobile applications:

- Lyfta: Gym Log Workout Tracker

- Macrofactor: Macro Tracker

- Mi Fitness (Xiaomi)

Make sure to include CLEAN and PRECISE illustrations of gym equipment. Any other illustrations or visualization should be well thought-out and correct.
